About Me

I'm Ignatius Nigel, an Aerospace Engineering graduate from KCG College of Technology, Chennai, with hands-on project experience across CAD design, CFD analysis, composite structures, and UAV systems.

I've taken multiple designs from concept through fabrication and flight-testing — from fixed-wing UAVs carrying 5 kg payloads to vacuum-bagged composite airframes — with results validated through both simulation and physical testing.

I'm quick to pick up new tools, comfortable working independently or in a team, and I care about seeing a design through to a working, flight-tested result — not just a report.
